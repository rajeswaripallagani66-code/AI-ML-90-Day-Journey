HR POLICY - COMPANY XYZ

1. LEAVE POLICY
- Casual Leave: 12 Casual Leaves per year. Maximum 3 casual leaves can be taken at a time. Casual leave cannot be carried forward. To apply, apply in HR portal at least 1 day before.
- Sick Leave: 8 Sick Leaves per year. Medical certificate required if more than 2 days. Sick leave cannot be combined with casual leave without manager approval. Half day sick leave is allowed.
- Earned Leave / Privilege Leave: Every employee gets 15 Earned Leaves (also called Privilege Leaves) per year. Can be carried forward up to 30 days. Apply at least 7 days before planned leave.
- Leave Carry Forward Rule: Casual leave cannot be carried forward. Earned Leave can be carried forward up to 30 days.
- Leave Balance: Check leave balance in HR portal > My Leaves. If leave balance is zero, you can apply for Leave Without Pay (LWP) with manager approval.
- Leave Without Pay (LWP): If no leave balance, LWP allowed with manager approval. LWP days are unpaid.
- Cancel Leave: To cancel leave, go to HR portal > My Leaves > Cancel before start date. Approved leaves can be cancelled only before start date.
- Leave in Probation: Leave is allowed in probation, but only Sick Leave and Casual Leave. Earned Leave not allowed in probation.
- Manager Approval: Manager can reject leave if team has critical work or if minimum staffing is not met. Manager must give reason in portal.

2. MATERNITY & PATERNITY LEAVE
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks paid maternity leave as per law. To apply, inform HR and manager 8 weeks before with doctor certificate, fill maternity form in HR portal.
- Paternity Leave: 10 days paid paternity leave within 30 days of child birth. Apply in HR portal with birth certificate.

3. WORKING HOURS & HOLIDAYS
- Working Hours: 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday. Core hours 10 AM to 4 PM must be available online.
- Saturday / Sunday: Saturday and Sunday are weekly off. Saturday is not a working day.
- Holiday List: Holiday list is published in January every year in HR portal > Holidays. Includes 12 public holidays + 2 optional holidays.

4. WFH POLICY
- WFH: 2 days WFH per week allowed with manager approval. Apply in HR portal 1 day before.
- WFH Timing: Core hours 10 AM to 4 PM must be available online.
- Full Week WFH: No. Only 2 days WFH per week are allowed.
- WFH in Probation: WFH is NOT allowed in probation period (first 6 months).
- WFH from Other City: WFH from other city allowed for max 5 days per month with manager + HR approval.
- Sick in WFH: If sick during WFH, apply for Sick Leave. Do not mark as WFH.
- Laptop for WFH: Company laptop is provided for WFH. Personal laptop not allowed for office work.
- Internet Allowance: Rs. 1000 per month internet allowance given for WFH employees.
- WFH Attendance: Mark WFH attendance in HR portal > Attendance > Mark WFH before 10 AM.

5. DRESS CODE
- Dress Code: Smart casual Monday to Thursday. Business casual on Friday. No shorts, slippers in office.

6. SALARY & BENEFITS
- Salary Slip: Salary slip is given on 5th of every month in HR portal > Payslips. To get salary slip, download from HR portal.
- Salary Credited: Salary is credited on last working day of every month to bank account.
- PF Contribution: PF contribution is 12% from employee + 12% from employer as per EPF Act.
- Medical Insurance: Medical insurance of Rs. 5 Lakhs for employee + family (spouse + 2 kids). Includes parents with extra premium.
- Travel Allowance: Rs. 2000 per month travel allowance for employees coming to office 15+ days.
- Bonus Policy: Annual bonus is 1 month salary, paid in April based on performance rating.
- Gratuity: Gratuity as per Gratuity Act, after 5 years of continuous service. 15 days salary per year of service.
- Appraisal: Appraisal happens once a year in April. Rating 1-5, increments based on rating.

7. NOTICE PERIOD & EXIT
- Probation Period: 6 months probation period.
- Notice Period: 30 days notice period during probation, 60 days notice period after confirmation. Same for resignation.
- Notice Period Buyout: Yes, you can buyout notice period by paying salary for remaining notice days, with manager + HR approval.
- Full and Final Settlement: Full and final settlement is done within 45 days of last working day. Includes pending salary, leave encashment, gratuity if eligible.
- Relieving Letter Process: After FNF, relieving letter is issued within 7 days. Request in HR portal > Exit.
- Exit Interview: Exit interview is mandatory with HR on last working day before relieving.
