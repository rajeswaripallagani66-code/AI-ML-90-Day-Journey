Casual Leave Policy:
- Every employee gets 12 Casual Leaves per year.
- Max 3 casual leaves can be taken at a time.
- Casual leave cannot be carried forward.

Sick Leave Policy:
- Every employee gets 8 Sick Leaves per year.
- Medical certificate required if more than 2 days.

Earned Leave Policy:
- Every employee gets 15 Earned Leaves per year.
- Can be carried forward up to 30 days.
- Apply at least 7 days before planned leave.

Maternity Leave Policy:
- 26 weeks paid maternity leave as per law.

Work From Home Policy:
- 2 days WFH per week allowed with manager approval.
- Core hours 10 AM to 4 PM must be available online.

Leave Balance:
Casual=12, Sick=8, Earned=15
